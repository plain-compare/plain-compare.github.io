The real monthly cost: sequencer vs credits vs mailboxes

Instantly’s bill is modular. Official packaging stacks as:

  1. Outreach — the sequencer (Growth $47 / Hypergrowth $97 / Light Speed $358 monthly). Caps emails and uploaded contacts. Unlimited connected accounts + warmup are in-plan.
  2. Instantly Credits — optional second subscription for SuperSearch / Copilot / AI agents (or buy a Bundle so both land on one invoice).
  3. Domains & DFY mailboxes — optional catalog prices above; not required to use your own providers’ inboxes.
  4. Add-ons — Outreach +$87/mo or Credits +$425/mo when you need more volume inside an eligible tier.

We are not inventing a “typical” total from guessed inbox counts. Your real cost is the plan(s) you pick plus any DFY/add-on line items you actually buy. Check the live pricing page before you budget a campaign.

FAQ

How much does Instantly cost per month?

Official Instantly Outreach self-serve plans checked 5 Sep 2026 are Growth $47/month, Hypergrowth $97/month, and Light Speed $358/month. Enterprise is custom. Annual Outreach as shown on instantly.ai/pricing: Growth $37.60, Hypergrowth $77.60, Light Speed $286.30 per month. Instantly Credits is a separate subscription (Nano $9 up). Bundles combine Outreach + Credits: Starter $94, Scale $194, Agency $555 monthly.

What is the Hypergrowth 100,000 vs 125,000 emails inconsistency?

pricing.md and the monthly Hypergrowth card print 125,000 emails/mo for the mid tier. The yearly Hypergrowth card, the on-page feature compare table, and the FAQ on instantly.ai/pricing print 100,000 emails for that same mid tier. This page states both figures and does not pick a side — verify the live pricing page toggle before you budget.

Does Instantly include a lead database in Outreach?

No. SuperSearch (vendor: 450M+ B2B contacts), Copilot, and AI agents run on Instantly Credits, a separate subscription from Outreach. Credits can also be bought inside Outreach + Credits bundles (Starter / Scale / Agency).

What do Instantly Credits cost?

Per pricing.md (5 Sep 2026): Free trial $0 / 100 credits; Nano $9 / 150; Growth $47–$77 / 1,500–2,000; Supersonic $97–$147 / 5,000–7,500; Hyper Credits from $197 / 10,000–200,000; Enterprise custom. Unused credits expire 2 months (monthly) or 1 year (annual) after purchase if unused.

Is there an Instantly free trial?

Yes. Instantly’s pricing.md states a 14-day free trial, no card required, with 250 uploaded contacts and 1,000 emails. Credits also list a free trial at $0 / 100 credits.

Do Instantly plans include unlimited email accounts and warmup?

Yes. Official packaging: all Outreach plans include unlimited email accounts, automated warmup, and API access. Webhooks start at Hypergrowth. SISR is included on Light Speed. Automations are included on all Email Outreach plans at no extra cost.

What are Instantly DFY mailbox and add-on prices?

From pricing.md: Domain $15/yr; Pre-warmed Google $10/mo; DFY Google $5/mo; DFY/Airmail $4/mo. Outreach add-on $87/mo (+25,000 contacts + 125,000 emails). Credits add-on $425/mo (+50,000 credits).

How does Instantly compare to Snov.io or Smartlead?

Instantly Outreach list prices start at Growth $47/mo. Official Snov.io Sales Suite (checked 5 Sep 2026): Starter $39, Pro S $99, Pro M $189, Pro L $369, Ultra $738 monthly. No hands-on bake-off and no AggregateRating on this page. No affiliate URLs.
