Pricing & real cost

List prices are not apples-to-apples. HighLevel’s $97 Starter matches Kartra’s Essentials/Starter neighborhood on paper, but HighLevel bills telecom and AI usage beside the plan — the wallet can swing a high-SMS month. Kartra’s FAQ claims flat-rate with no extra fees for storage or email sends, while Essentials still prints a 5% transaction fee on sales (Starter+ print 0%). Payment-processor fees sit under both. We will not invent a “typical monthly bill” without metering either account.

Funnels & page builder

Both sell funnel + page builders. HighLevel lists website & funnel builder, Funnel AI, forms/surveys/quizzes, and domain management among core features. Kartra lists funnel mapper on Essentials+, funnel simulation from Growth, and drag-and-drop pages (5-page cap on Essentials; unlimited on Starter+). We did not time a rebuild or split-test conversion rates for this page.

Email, SMS & automation

HighLevel: unified conversations, email + SMS marketing*, inbound/outbound calling*, workflow automation — with usage-based charges on starred items. Unlimited/Pro can rebill usage to clients (markup on Pro). Kartra: Essentials caps email at 10,000/mo on the plan card (flag above); Starter+ print unlimited email & SMS; Growth+ adds automations and affiliate management. Contact ceilings on Kartra are hard plan limits with auto-upgrade language if leads exceed the tier.

Memberships, courses & checkouts

Kartra’s packaging is explicit for creators: 1 membership/course on Essentials, unlimited on Starter+, plus checkouts and (Starter+) video hosting. HighLevel lists Courses and Communities on the core features list and payments/invoicing with Stripe, PayPal, and others — without printing a membership count per plan on the cards we captured. Pick Kartra on paper if course/membership delivery is the primary job; pick HighLevel if CRM + client messaging + sub-accounts dominate.

Who should pick which

  • Agency / SaaS resale: GoHighLevel Unlimited or Agency Pro — sub-accounts, rebill, SaaS Mode on Pro.
  • Solo creator / coach launching a course: Kartra Starter or Growth — memberships, checkouts, contact caps you can budget.
  • Tiny budget test: Kartra Essentials ($59) is the lowest monthly floor listed here — watch the 5% txn fee and 500-contact / page caps.
  • Heavy SMS / calling: Model HighLevel’s usage wallet before assuming $97 is the bill; Kartra’s unlimited email/SMS cards still sit behind contact tiers.
