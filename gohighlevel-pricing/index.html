The real monthly cost: usage sits beside the plan

GoHighLevel’s own FAQ asks “Are there any hidden fees?” and answers: the subscription covers platform features, and there are usage-based costs for telecommunications and AI. Unlimited and Pro can rebill those costs to clients. Only Pro is described as adding markup.

Per-message and per-minute rates change. We are not copying a third-party rate card as if we metered it. Optional add-ons on the official page (examples): AI Employee $50 or $97 per sub-account per month, branded client portal $49, HIPAA $297, white-label mobile app $497, premium support $500.

Which plan for which business

  • Starter ($97): you, or you plus a couple of client accounts. Hard stop at 3 sub-accounts.
  • Unlimited ($297): an agency adding clients without buying a new seat each time. Rebill usage at cost.
  • Agency Pro ($497): sell HighLevel as your own SaaS, auto-create accounts, and mark up phone/email.
