Which plan for which creator

  • Basic ($179 / $143 annual): one small offer stack — hard stop at 5 products and 2,500 contacts. Two admin seats.
  • Growth ($249 / $199 annual): growing course/community catalog (50 products, 25k contacts) plus 500 AI credits and 11 admin users.
  • Pro ($499 / $399 annual): unlimited products, 100k contacts, branded app, API, three communities/websites, 1,000 AI credits. Worth the jump when you need the app/API or contact/product ceiling.

FAQ

How much does Kajabi cost per month?

Official Kajabi self-serve plans checked 5 Sep 2026 are Basic $179/month, Growth $249/month, and Pro $499/month. On annual billing the site shows $143, $199, and $399 per month respectively. Payment processing and optional AI credit packs sit on top.

What is included on every Kajabi plan?

According to Kajabi’s Compare features table, Access Groups, Funnels, Landing Pages, and Marketing Emails are Unlimited on Basic, Growth, and Pro.

What are Kajabi payment processing fees?

For US entities using Kajabi Payments: Basic 2.9%+$0.30, Growth 2.8%+$0.30, Pro 2.7%+$0.30. Third-party payment providers (excluding PayPal and Kajabi Payments) add 2% / 1% / 0.5% by plan.

Does Kajabi include a branded mobile app?

The branded mobile app is included on Pro. Kajabi’s pricing page markets it as a $199/mo value. API access is also included on Pro (page markets $25/mo value).

Is there a Kajabi free trial?

Yes. Kajabi markets a Start Free Trial / 30-day free trial on the pricing page. The page also shows Save 50% promo CTAs; treat those as marketing CTAs and verify current offer terms on kajabi.com/pricing before budgeting.
