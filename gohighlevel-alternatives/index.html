The 7 alternatives

1. Systeme.io — best budget all-in-one

Official FAQ on systeme.io/pricing (checked 5 Sep 2026, PT; FAQ “last updated: April 2026”): Free (2,000 contacts, 3 funnels, 1 course), Startup $17/mo (5,000 contacts), Webinar $47/mo (10,000 contacts + automated webinars), Unlimited $97/mo (unlimited contacts, sub-accounts, free migration). All plans claim unlimited email sends and 0% transaction fees via Stripe/PayPal.

Who it’s for: Solo marketers who want funnels + email + courses without a HighLevel wallet. Not for: agencies that need HighLevel-style SaaS Mode markup of phone/SMS.

See Systeme.io pricing (official link only).

2. Kartra — best for creators selling courses

Official kartra.com/pricing (5 Sep 2026, PT): Essentials $59/mo ($52/mo annual) — 500 contacts, 10K emails/mo on plan cards (one compare table also shows 1,000 — verify live), 5% transaction fees; Starter $119/mo ($99 annual) — 2,500 contacts, unlimited email/SMS, 0% fees; Growth $229/mo ($189 annual) — 12,500 contacts; Professional $549/mo ($429 annual) — 25,000 contacts. 30-day money-back language on the page.

Who it’s for: Coaches and info-product sellers consolidating pages, email, memberships. Not for: agencies living in client sub-accounts and telecom rebilling.

Related: GoHighLevel vs Kartra. See Kartra pricing (official link only — no Kartra affiliate).

3. ClickFunnels — best funnel-first stack

Official clickfunnels.com/pricing (5 Sep 2026, PT): Launch $97, Scale $197, Optimize $297 monthly; annual shown ~$81 / ~$164 / ~$248 per month; Dominate $5,997/yr only. Workspaces 1 / 5 / 10 / 20. Example email caps: 10K/50K, 75K/300K, 150K/750K, 400K/1.2M contacts/emails-mo. FAQ: no revenue share of your sales; 14-day trial + 30-day money-back language.

Who it’s for: Offer builders who want predictable contact/email ceilings. Full vs page: ClickFunnels vs GoHighLevel.

4. HubSpot — best when CRM hubs beat an agency OS

HubSpot publishes free foundational CRM tools and paid Marketing / Sales / Service hubs with seat and marketing-contact pricing on hubspot.com/pricing. Dynamic price widgets often fail automated fetches — do not treat any third-party HubSpot fee table as fact until you confirm live numbers on HubSpot’s site.

Who it’s for: Teams that outgrow all-in-one agency tooling and want hub-based CRM + marketing. Not for: operators who need HighLevel’s native SMS/calling wallet and SaaS Mode in one flat agency plan.

See HubSpot pricing (official link only).

5. Vendasta — best agency resale / marketplace play

Official vendasta.com/pricing (5 Sep 2026, PT): platform subscription can bill $0 when you hit monthly product minimums. Plan minimums: Starter $99/mo (1 seat, 10 Snapshot Reports), Professional $499/mo (5 seats, white-label portal, 1-year contract), Premium $999/mo (10 seats, multi-location), plus Custom Enterprise. Payments processing called out at 2.9% + $0.30. 14-day free trial language on the page.

Who it’s for: Agencies reselling local SEO, reputation, and AI workforce products. Different model than HighLevel’s self-serve SaaS Mode.

See Vendasta pricing (official link only).

6. Keap — best SMB automation with hands-on setup

Official keap.com/pricing (5 Sep 2026, PT): platform starting at $299/mo (also shown as $2,988/year). Starts with 2 user licenses; additional users $39/mo each. Implementation packages are required (vendor FAQ). Text marketing: Tier 1 (500 messages / 100 voice minutes) included; higher tiers from $24/mo; overages $0.015/message and $0.01/minute (U.S.). Annual contracts: $299 early termination fee if cancelled early (FAQ).

Who it’s for: Small businesses that want CRM + email/SMS automation and will pay for implementation. Not for: DIY agencies optimizing for the lowest software-only sticker.

See Keap pricing (official link only).

7. Zoho CRM — best per-user CRM value

Official zoho.com/crm pricing: Free forever for 3 users. Paid Standard / Professional / Enterprise editions are per-user (currency selector on-page). The same page states Zoho CRM Plus starts at $20/user/month. We are not inventing USD figures for Standard/Pro/Enterprise tiers when the fetch locale is ambiguous. Zoho is a CRM-led suite, not a drop-in HighLevel messaging + SaaS Mode clone.

Who it’s for: Sales teams that want CRM depth and Zoho ecosystem pricing. Pair with other tools if you need full funnel + SMS agency OS.

See Zoho CRM pricing (official link only).
