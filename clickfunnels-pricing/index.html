Processor fees beside the plan

ClickFunnels FAQ: software takes no percentage of sales. Payments AI (included app) still has processor fees — US gateway 2.9% + $0.30, plus documented extras for non-US cards, connected processors, subscriptions, and automation. See Payments AI docs; we do not invent a blended take-home.
