The real monthly cost: processor fees sit beside the plan

The ClickFunnels pricing FAQ asks “Does ClickFunnels take a percentage of my sales?” and answers: No. You’ll keep every penny you make. That is software revenue share — not card processing. Which plan for which operator

  • Launch ($97 / $81 annual): one brand getting started — hard stop at 1 workspace, 2 seats, 10K contacts, 50K emails/mo, 3 courses, 5 custom domains. No custom code or API on the official table.
  • Scale ($197 / $164 annual): official “Most Popular.” Five workspaces, 75K contacts, unlimited custom domains, custom code, webhooks/API, priority + live chat. The jump if Launch’s 1-workspace / no-API ceiling is the blocker.
  • Optimize ($297 / $248 annual): vendor copy: “marketers running high-converting funnels at scale.” 10 workspaces, 150K contacts, 10 courses/communities, advanced workflows, Affiliate Center, private 1-hour onboarding.
  • Dominate ($5,997/yr only): 20 workspaces/seats, 400K contacts, VIP support, 3-hour private onboarding, plus Dominate-only SSO, dedicated email IP, branding removal, certification training, and an account manager. Demo / “Dominate Now” CTAs, not the same Start Free Trial button.

Named recommendation (packaging only)

On paper, Scale is the plan ClickFunnels itself labels Most Popular, and it is the first self-serve tier that unlocks custom code, webhooks/API, and unlimited custom domains. Stay on Launch if one workspace and 10K contacts are enough. Jump to Optimize when you need 10 workspaces, Affiliate Center, or advanced workflows. Treat Dominate as the annual VIP / 20-workspace package, not a monthly experiment. This is official packaging, not a bake-off we ran.

FAQ

How much does ClickFunnels cost per month?

Official ClickFunnels self-serve plans checked 30 Aug 2026 are Launch $97/month, Scale $197/month, and Optimize $297/month. On annual billing the site shows $81, $164, and $248 per month respectively. Dominate is $5,997 per year only (no monthly option on the pricing page). Processor fees from Payments AI sit beside the software plan.

What is included on every ClickFunnels plan?

The official comparison table lists unlimited funnels, unlimited visitors, unlimited site pages, and unlimited landing pages on Launch, Scale, Optimize, and Dominate. Hosting is included (FAQ: no separate hosting account). Payments AI is listed as an included app. Plan cards also mark “Unlimited Funnels” as Limited Time — treat that as a current packaging caveat, not a forever guarantee.

Does ClickFunnels take a percentage of my sales?

ClickFunnels’ own pricing FAQ answers: No. You’ll keep every penny you make. That is about software revenue share, not card processing. If you take payments through Payments AI, processor and gateway fees still apply (see Payments AI docs).

What extra fees sit beside the ClickFunnels plan?

The software subscription is the plan price. Payments AI (included as an app on every plan) publishes US gateway transaction fees of 2.9% + $0.30, 0% gateway fee on the Payments AI gateway, and 1% gateway fee plus the other processor’s fees if you connect Stripe, PayPal, Coinbase, or Klarna. Recurring subscriptions add 0.5% per recurring transaction; automation rules add 0.5% (bundled, not doubled, if you use both). Contact and email overage prices are not listed on clickfunnels.com/pricing — omitted here.

Is there a ClickFunnels free trial or money-back guarantee?

Launch, Scale, and Optimize show Start Free Trial CTAs. The pricing-page FAQ states a 30-day money-back guarantee. The same page’s FAQ schema.org block describes a 14-day free trial plus that 30-day window after the trial ends (44 days total). Dominate uses Dominate Now / Book A Demo CTAs, not the same Start Free Trial button. Verify current trial terms on clickfunnels.com/pricing before you budget.

Are ClickFunnels funnels really unlimited?

The feature table lists Funnels as Unlimited on all four plans. The plan cards add the words “Unlimited Funnels Limited Time.” We do not treat unlimited as a permanent entitlement beyond what the live page currently prints.

What is the difference between Launch, Scale, Optimize, and Dominate?

Launch is 1 workspace, 2 team members, 10K contacts, 50K emails/mo, 3 courses / 3 communities, 5 custom domains, 1 store, standard support, 1M AI words. Scale (labeled Most Popular) is 5 workspaces, 5 seats, 75K contacts, 300K emails/mo, 6/6 courses/community, unlimited custom domains, 5 stores, priority + live chat, custom code, webhooks/API, 2M AI words. Optimize is 10/10 workspaces/seats, 150K contacts, 750K emails/mo, 10/10 courses/community, 10 stores, 5M AI words, plus a private 1-hour onboarding call. Dominate is annual-only at $5,997/yr: 20/20 workspaces/seats, 400K contacts, 1.2M emails/mo, 20/20 courses/community, 20 stores, 10M AI words, VIP support, private 3-hour onboarding, plus Dominate-only rows for dedicated email IP, SSO, branding removal, certification training, and an account manager.

Can I use a custom domain, and do I need hosting?

Yes, custom domains are supported. Launch includes 5; Scale, Optimize, and Dominate list unlimited custom domains. If you do not have a domain, ClickFunnels says it will give you a free yourdomain.myclickfunnels.com subdomain. All plans include hosting (FAQ: no separate hosting account; 40 edge servers). You can change plans at any time, including during or after the trial.
