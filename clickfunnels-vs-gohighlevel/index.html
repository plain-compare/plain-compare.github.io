Where ClickFunnels wins (on paper)

  • Funnel-first product story — sales funnels, editor, A/B testing, smart checkout, countdown funnels are front-and-center.
  • Bundled email ceilings — contacts and emails/month are plan numbers you can budget without a separate telecom wallet (still respect the caps).
  • Training ladder — Knowledgebase / FunnelFlix tiers by plan on the official comparison table.
  • Money-back language — vendor FAQ describes a post-trial 30-day refund window in addition to the trial.

Where GoHighLevel wins (on paper)

  • Agency multi-client depth — unlimited sub-accounts from Unlimited up; SaaS Mode and markup rebill on Agency Pro.
  • Native phone / SMS / calling — messaging is a first-class platform feature (with usage fees).
  • CRM + calendars + workflows packaged as the core agency OS, not an add-on afterthought.
  • Unlimited contacts and users called out across self-serve plans on the official page.

Who should pick which

Choose ClickFunnels if: you live in funnel launches, course/community offers, and want plan-level email/contact caps without standing up a HighLevel-style usage wallet.

Choose GoHighLevel if: you run (or sell) client accounts, need SMS/calling in the same login as CRM and funnels, or want SaaS Mode-style resale.

Skip both (for now) if: you only need a single landing page — a lighter page builder may be enough.

FAQ

Is ClickFunnels or GoHighLevel better in 2026?

It depends on the job. ClickFunnels is stronger as a funnel-first builder with bundled email limits and workspaces. GoHighLevel is stronger as an agency CRM + messaging + funnels stack. List prices are not apples-to-apples because HighLevel usage sits beside the plan.

How much does ClickFunnels cost vs GoHighLevel?

ClickFunnels monthly (5 Sep 2026): Launch $97, Scale $197, Optimize $297; Dominate $5,997/year. GoHighLevel: Starter $97, Unlimited $297, Agency Pro $497, plus usage for phone/SMS/email/AI.

Which is better for agencies?

GoHighLevel’s Unlimited and Agency Pro packaging (unlimited sub-accounts, rebill, SaaS Mode on Pro) matches multi-client agencies more closely. ClickFunnels scales workspaces (1–20) and may still win for funnel-centric shops.

Does ClickFunnels include email and CRM?

Yes per the official pricing feature table: email contact/send caps by plan, CRM pipelines, Message Hub, appointments, and related apps.

Are there hidden fees on either platform?

ClickFunnels says it takes no percentage of sales; watch plan caps and Payments AI processor fees. GoHighLevel says usage for telecom and AI is separate from the subscription.

Can I try both before paying?

Both advertise trials (ClickFunnels free trial + money-back FAQ language; GoHighLevel 14-day trial). We have not metered paid accounts for this article.

Should I switch from ClickFunnels to GoHighLevel?

Switch if you need native SMS/calling, many sub-accounts, or SaaS Mode. Stay if funnel building with clear email caps is enough. We have not timed a migration.
